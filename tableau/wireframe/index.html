Executive View, v0.7 sketch

Onboarding Reimagined | work in progress, a starting point for discussion | Oct 2026

A draft of what an onboarding dashboard could show. The measures and layout don't depend on a particular tool: Tableau, Looker or Power BI could all build it. Dashboard work starts March 4, once the new survey questions are live.

Phase 1 possible by March, data exists or flows through Helix   TBC data exists, path into Helix unclear   Later needs data that isn't captured yet (around 2028)

What changed since v0.6:

  • Every tile shows its phase: what's possible by March and what waits for later.
  • Survey results already flow from Glint into Helix, so survey-based tiles are Phase 1.
  • Filters use a Start Cohort (month or quarter) and Talent Type (job level 2: executive or emerging talent).
  • The first survey wave is Week 2. Productivity is a 5-point manager rating at Day 90, shown on a 0 to 100 scale.
  • On-time start becomes Start Date Held plus the days shifted when a date changes. Hiring managers gain a Day 1 Manager Meeting tile.
  • Equipment moves to TBC until its Power BI source and owner are confirmed. A new section lists where each measure comes from.

  • Possible framing: two questions, three tiles. Start Date Held and Onboarding QA ask whether PEX is delivering onboarding well. Cases per New Hire asks whether the new experience makes PEX's job easier: if the portal works, people need fewer tickets.
  • No blended PEX score. These use different units, and some go the other way (lower is better), so a blend would hide which one slipped. The header shows how many tiles are at goal.
  • PEX's own targets carry over, so PEX and this dashboard agree on what good looks like.
  • Start the before picture early. Cases per new hire and HR-raised % exist today, so the comparison after the portal launches has a baseline.

5. Proposed survey scoring (open to change)

  • 5-point agree scale for every rated question. Strongly disagree to strongly agree.
  • Favorable = 4 or 5. That's Glint's standard, so our numbers match what Glint shows.
  • Bucket score = average of its questions' % favorable, each question weighted equally. Keep Glint's 0 to 100 score in the tooltip for anyone comparing to Glint reports.
  • One wave at a time. Week 2, Day 30 and Day 90 are scored separately. Averaging them would hide the trend across the first 90 days.
  • Hide small groups. No score under 5 responses, the Glint default (the floor is 3). Comments need 10.
  • 3 or more questions per bucket where we can. One or two questions read as an item, not an index. Once real data exists, check the questions hang together (Cronbach's alpha 0.70 or higher).
  • Use Glint's wording where it fits. Standard items keep results comparable over time and with other companies.
  • Show n and response rate on every tile. A 90% from 6 people isn't a 90% from 600.
  • Comments aren't scored. Barriers stay in Glint's comment reports.
  • Targets after a baseline. Run two quarters, then set targets per bucket.
  • Overall Onboarding Score is a weighted average of about 8 or 9 questions. The single "overall experience" question can also show on its own.
  • Productivity is a 5-point manager rating at Day 90, converted to 0 to 100 like Glint scores. Watch the change from Day 30, not the Day 30 level.
