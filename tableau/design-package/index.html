Categorical palettes (Format › Edit Colors)

Default = Tableau 10, assigned in fixed order. Keep a series' color constant across every sheet (Tableau does this per field automatically once assigned). "Color Blind" (Tableau, 10) is the accessible alternative. Validator check on Tableau 10: red/green slots 3 & 5 collapse for deuteranopes, and orange, teal, yellow, pink and gray are below 3:1 on white — so always direct-label or pair with a legend, and keep categories ≤ 6.
